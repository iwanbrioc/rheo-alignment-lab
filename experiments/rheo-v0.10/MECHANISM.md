## Motivation, not a newly established benchmark failure

The user identifies a plausible weakness: the place where a difficulty is
experienced, its generating restriction, and the person's available agency may
differ. The present v0.9 asks about generating restrictions but does not explicitly
represent competing explanations, system agency or their separate confidence.
This is a design hypothesis, not evidence that v0.9 failed a new benchmark.

## Mechanism and intervention

RWB remains the seven canonical reciprocal triplets, used only where material.
Marmot-inspired social-determinants questions test upstream conditions without
assuming individual disadvantage. Active Inference inspires explicit observations,
competing explanations, predictions and revision; no mathematical FEP or Bayesian
probabilities are implemented. Rheocracy supplies minimum sufficient influence,
real agency, regeneration, power/exit and burden checks.

Versioned schemas, prompts, validation and endpoints live in this directory.
The existing server_v0_9.mjs, schemas, prompts and completed research remain intact.
Mobile keeps three alternatives, explicit choice, local context and legacy history.
Private gift/pathway notes are a separate product feature, not evaluation evidence.

## Predictions registered before development probes

- Primary: fewer actions that treat upstream constraints as personal deficits;
  better separation of generating restriction, available agency and system agency.
- Collateral: more useful discriminating questions, better provenance and ordinal
  confidence explanations, fewer inaccessible actions and hidden burden transfers.
- Null: no automatic improvement in factual knowledge, physical service access,
  wellbeing, provider availability or structural conditions. Good outcomes alone
  do not validate RWB, Marmot integration or Active Inference.
- Regression risks: structural overreach, false alternatives, long answers,
  hesitation on urgent needs, higher latency/cost and forced ontology. Direct
  cases must remain simple; insufficient evidence may justify no primary hypothesis.

## Development checks

New synthetic/adversarial cases A-F: upstream restriction mistaken for personal
deficit; genuinely individual/actionable uncertainty; generating restriction and
agency at different horizons; inaccessible recommendation; burden displacement;
sparse evidence. Additional checks cover narrator motive inference, area-to-person
inference, coercion, adversarial instructions, competing predictions and revisions.
These are developer-visible diagnostic probes, not a blinded or confirmatory study.
Record failures before amendments; do not revise this record after seeing outputs.

## Operational confidence

Low: a live possibility with weak, indirect or missing discriminating evidence.
Medium: some case-specific support, but a material rival or evidence gap remains.
High: strong case-specific support and a stated reason the main rivals fit less
well; still revisable. Confidence in a reported event is distinct from confidence
in its cause. Ordinals are not calibrated probabilities or wellbeing scores.

## Scope and limits

No scores, ranking, psychotypes, deprivation inference, coercive giving, public
map, mesh networking, cloud accounts or new external area-data provider.
