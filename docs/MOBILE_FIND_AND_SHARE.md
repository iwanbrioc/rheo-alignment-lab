# Rheo — Find and Share interface

The native app now opens with **Find** rather than the conceptual mycelium.
Two primary actions lead: **Find what I need** focuses the search field;
**Share a resource** opens a short resource form. Persistent navigation has four
labelled destinations: Find, Map, My day, Nearby. Changing tabs returns to the top.

Essentials shortcuts cover Food, Water, Warmth, Shelter and Care. Other shortcuts
cover gardens, company, arts/music and skills/tools. Local search matches recorded
names, descriptions and contact labels; optional distance breaks ties among
matching records. It is deterministic, on-device and requires neither AI nor an
internet connection. It does not infer availability, suitability or safe travel.
Distances are straight-line estimates, not routing instructions or trust scores.
Search currently uses English keyword shortcuts; free text searches the original
record text. No language understanding, inventory confirmation or emergency
service coverage is claimed.

Find excludes fictional examples entirely. No-results states report the absence
of matching saved records and offer nearby exchange or resource creation, without
suggesting that a service exists. The sample remains an explicit map/data action.

A result opens its details directly in one modal. Practical access text and host
information come first. A phone number or email supplied as the contact can open
the system dialler/mail composer on an explicit tap; the app neither calls nor
sends a message automatically. Located portals offer a map view. Recorded
relationships link directly to related resources. Management, manual text-token
exchange and edit controls are collapsed behind **Manage this portal**.

The resource form asks what is offered, how someone can access it, its category,
an optional host/contact and optional approximate location. Exact coordinates and
resource type are under extra options. Sharing remains explicit; new/edited
records start private. It does not publish a private shelter, contact or location
merely because someone saves it.

System typography, stronger text contrast, larger buttons and persistent labelled
tabs replace the introductory manifesto and navigation chips. The conceptual
network, offline geographic view, calendar handoff, matrix and embedded nearby
transport remain accessible. There is no dependency, native module, research
behaviour or transport-protocol change in this interface update.

Verification: typecheck, existing local/session smoke tests, 29 mesh/discovery
checks, and iOS Hermes export. Physical iPhone rendering, large-text/VoiceOver
interaction and usability with people under stress still require device tests;
this is not evidence that the app is validated for conflict-zone safety.
