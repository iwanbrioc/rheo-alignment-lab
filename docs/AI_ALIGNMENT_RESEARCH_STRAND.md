# AI alignment research strand — proposed design specification
24 November 2026. Research-only extension; no runtime changes, no revision to frozen v0.2 claims, no AI added to the v0.3 field map.

## Scope
RWB supplies unscored contextual lenses. Rheocracy supplies contestable, locally situated procedures. Rheo tests whether those mechanisms add measurable value beyond strong generic deliberation. The v0.2 Guide and Research Evaluator remain separate; the v0.3 local-first field prototype remains deterministic.

## Requirements
- Preserve meaningful refusal, exit, correction, uncertainty and provenance; do not require trust accumulation for essentials.
- Expose costs to absent parties and ecological systems, without asserting unverified facts or imputing consent.
- Prefer proportionate, reversible interventions where safe; do not delay protective action or preserve coercive relationships.
- Distinguish factual improvement, participant interpretation and influence on reporting; permit truthful education and emotional support.
- Keep no-self and wellbeing outside aggregate live optimisation; evaluation-only predicates may be computed in controlled cases.
- Maintain human authority over conflicting values; participation and perspective-taking must be voluntary, accessible and contestable.
- Treat signed records as integrity evidence, not proof of truth or identity.
- Keep development, frozen and external sealed cases separate. Apply existing mechanism-change rules before any prompt alteration.

## Work packages
WP1: normative specification and dialogue with ARC; explore computable toy-world predicates rather than claim mechanistic guarantees.
WP2: causal feedback benchmark; compare same-model ordinary, sham and Rheo conditions with independent state-transition scoring.
WP3: participatory governance; explore consent-based veil/reveal/revise sessions with CIP or independent partners, preserving dissent and exit. No claim that simulated stakeholder voices constitute consent.
WP4: flourishing and contextual agency research; invite FLIP to critique operational definitions and study design.
WP5: independent replication, ethical review for human studies and publication of null or adverse results.

## Acceptance gates
Design reviewed; benchmark preregistered; independent sealed cases; reliable blinded scoring; incremental benefit against sham with no unacceptable safety regressions; external replication. No claim of production readiness, clinical benefit or frontier AI safety from this strand alone.
