Exchange a shared map

Export only records you chose to share. Transfer the file directly, then import it on another device. Signatures are checked before records are merged.

What sharing means

Exported information can be copied and relayed. Share only locations and details you have permission to distribute. Withdrawing a record signals a change when peers reconnect; it cannot erase copies already held elsewhere.

Bluetooth bridge

Checking browser support…

Requires a peripheral implementing the Rheo GATT protocol. This browser does not advertise or relay a background phone-to-phone mesh.

The next transport layer

Native phone relays will use the same signed packets with discovery, bounded forwarding and reconnect recovery. The bridge interface is implemented here; native relay hardware is not included in this version.

Trust has a context

Confirm a particular opportunity, skill or care commitment. An endorsement belongs to a specific revision and expires. A signature identifies its source; it does not establish that the claim is true.

Device fingerprint

This device’s signing key stays in browser storage. Losing that storage loses this identity. Exported maps do not back up the key.

↗

Rules you can inspect

Availability comes first, then distance. Expiry, capacity, dependency conflicts and consent remain visible. Popularity and trust totals never determine a person’s worth or access to basic help.

How pathways are evaluated
  • Expired, paused, empty or conflicting opportunities need renewal or repair.
  • Missing dependencies block a pathway; unresolved enabling conditions require confirmation.
  • Consent remains necessary even when endorsements exist.
  • Participation records describe experience; they never spend or mint trust.
  • No Self remains an unscored lens.
