Foundational conditions

Meaning cannot be minted. No investor token, exchange, financial return or reward for compassion. Central funding only tapers when equivalent local support is present. A community remains free to rely on appropriate long-term public or philanthropic support.

Legal characterisation, charity status, Gift Aid, grants and curator taxation are outside this simulated prototype.
