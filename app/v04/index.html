What is — and isn't — connected

Working now: Local portal creation, a living network graph, offline browser storage, import/export and Bitchat message handoff by copy and paste. The message codec includes a small, tested adaptation of Bitchat's TLV text-payload framing.

Not yet: Direct Bluetooth access from Rheo on iPhone, unattended phone-to-phone forwarding, peer authentication or secure encryption of shared tokens. Bitchat carries the pasted message across its own network; Rheo cannot independently confirm delivery.

Privacy: Tokens are readable, unsigned demo-format data. Never share private addresses, identities or sensitive information through this prototype. Receiving a token is not evidence of the sender's trustworthiness.
