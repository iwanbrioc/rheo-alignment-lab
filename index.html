FROM PLACES TO POSSIBILITIES

A map of relationships,
and a day to move through them.

Every portal is an invitation. The mycelium shows connections people have explicitly recorded between portals — shared resources, skills, care and work. Nothing is automatically endorsed because it is nearby or popular.

The Time/Space Matrix, inspired by Open Space Technology, brings activities into one view: spaces × time. Choose your own journey, change your mind, leave when you need to, and make room for whatever emerges.

THE RECIPROCAL ECONOMY · PROPOSED MODEL

From speculative gains to shared capacity.

The tithe is envisioned as a voluntary transfer from realised gains to the commons. Its purpose would be to support a temporary, tapered Curator Basic Income, helping local curators establish resilient pathways without making other people's need a revenue stream.

It is not a tradable Rheo coin, a savings product, a guaranteed return or an operational investment offer. Fund governance, eligibility, treasury controls, legal and tax treatment, and safeguards must be developed before accepting contributions.

WHAT IS WORKING NOW

Prototype, not promise.

Rheo Mesh v0.4 runs without an AI API. You can browse ten fictional portals, explore their recorded mycelial connections, propose your own and follow a sample Open Space day.

Local storage, JSON exchange and a manual handoff to the separate Bitchat app are provided for experimentation. The Bitchat-derived payload codec does not make Rheo a native Bluetooth mesh client: there is no automatic iPhone-to-iPhone radio connection yet. Solar relay nodes are on hold.

Data in this prototype is unverified and is not suitable for sensitive or hostile-surveillance environments. Development of the curator pathway and tithe remains exploratory.
