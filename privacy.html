Privacy notes

Rheo Mesh v0.4 is an experimental browser prototype. Its working features have a different data model from the earlier AI-powered Rheo mobile alpha.

This website

rheocracy.org is an informational website hosted on GitHub Pages. The public landing pages contain no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, account forms or cookies. GitHub and network providers may process connection information such as IP addresses to serve the website. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Rheo Mesh v0.4

The Rheo Mesh v0.4 prototype runs its portals, time/space matrix and My Path logic on your device without submitting questions to an AI service. It stores locally added portals, connections, activities and chosen sessions in your browser's localStorage; it is not an encrypted vault. Data may be available to anyone with access to your device or browser backups. Clearing your browser's site data can erase it.

The sample portals, connections and sessions are all fictional; they are not real-world claims. The positions in the mycelium illustration are conceptual and do not require GPS. No account, cloud sync, background location or server database is used by this prototype.

Sharing and Bitchat

v0.4 lets you explicitly export a JSON file or prepare a short RHEO4: text token for a portal you have marked shareable. You may paste that token into the separate Bitchat application. Rheo itself does not send it over Bluetooth. Tokens are not signed or encrypted; import labels them unverified and does not prove that the sender or information is trustworthy. Once you share data with someone else, Rheo cannot erase their copy.

Do not use this prototype to circulate private addresses, personal information, sensitive affiliations, access schedules or material that could put someone at risk. It is not ready for environments with hostile surveillance or for emergency communication.

Older AI-powered alpha

The separate, older Rheo mobile decision companion has a different privacy model: asking for AI recommendations can send question and selected context through the Rheo server to an AI provider. Local-context searches and voice recognition may also involve external services. These features are not part of the v0.4 browser prototype linked from the current homepage.

Further development

A future native iPhone version incorporating automatic BLE exchange would require additional platform permissions, an updated threat model, end-to-end interoperability testing, secure records, revocation behaviour and a fuller privacy review before any sensitive pilot. Solar relay devices are not part of this version.

Contact

For information or privacy questions email info@rheocracy.org. Ordinary email should not be used for sensitive personal accounts or confidential situations.
