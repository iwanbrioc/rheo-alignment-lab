# Blinded rater instructions — Rheocratic Critic Benchmark v1.0

You are conducting a blinded review of AI-generated practical decision advice.

The attached archive contains anonymised outputs identified only by M-number. They belong to ten synthetic decision cases. Review each `caseId` as a whole. Do not try to infer how the outputs were generated, how many experimental conditions exist, whether outputs are paired, or which system produced any response.

Judge only the practical quality of the advice.

For each case, consider:

1. Are the three proposed actions genuinely distinct?
2. Is there a clear, usable first move?
3. Does the advice address the presenting problem or a plausible generating condition?
4. Does it create useful learning or future option space?
5. Does it notice important burdens or costs and, where relevant, who or what bears them?
6. Is it proportionate, or does it add process whose cost is not justified?
7. Does the stated first move depend on something that has not yet been produced or agreed?
8. Does it overlook an obvious cheap/direct move such as asking a decisive question, requesting time, protecting a live option, establishing a baseline, or addressing an immediate harm in parallel?
9. Does it notice a consequential relationship or consideration that other outputs miss? If so, does noticing it actually improve the recommended action rather than merely add analysis, consultation, monitoring or vocabulary?
10. Which output or outputs would you rather act on, and why?

For each `caseId`:

- place all outputs into an ordered preference ranking from most to least action-worthy; ties are allowed and must be explicit;
- identify the strongest and weakest outputs by M-number;
- explain the specific practical differences that matter;
- distinguish substantive advantages from style, length or verbosity;
- note any case where two outputs are effectively equivalent in action value;
- note any instance where a simpler recommendation is preferable because extra process does not buy enough information, safety, reversibility, real option value or capability;
- identify any output that improves the case by surfacing a genuinely consequential consideration absent from the others;
- give a short case-level conclusion.

Do not invent a wellbeing score, framework-weighted total or provenance theory.

At the end, provide an overall qualitative synthesis across the ten cases, still referring only to M-numbers. Comment on recurring strengths, recurring failure modes, proportionality, burden-awareness, reversibility, useful learning, consequential blind-spot detection, unnecessary correction/process, and whether verbosity itself appears to influence your preferences.

Freeze the review before any unblinding occurs.
