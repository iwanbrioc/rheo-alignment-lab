# v1.0 case-design notes — frozen before confirmatory generation

These notes are for mechanism interpretation after unblinding. They are not supplied to the optimiser, critics, finaliser or raters.

The benchmark deliberately mixes cases where a reciprocal critic may have something distinctive to notice with ordinary cases where a competent neutral optimiser should already be sufficient. This is necessary to test both **sensitivity** and **abstention discipline**.

## Prospectively expected critic-opportunity cases

These are not guaranteed Rheocratic wins. They merely contain a plausible reciprocal relationship whose omission could matter.

- **SYN-301 — Rural demand-responsive bus pilot:** nominal service availability may not be real affordance for current users; external satisfaction data may omit people excluded by the proposed infrastructure.
- **SYN-302 — Volunteer-run theatre expansion:** current willingness to cover may be mistaken for regenerative capacity; absence of complaint may hide lived depletion.
- **SYN-303 — Housing retrofit consultation:** residents can speak extensively while the issue they care most about is structurally unable to influence the decision; voice may not be consequential dialogue.
- **SYN-304 — Library savings choice:** the branch-A-or-branch-B frame may be a constructed option set rather than the material constraint; the actual constraint is recurring savings.
- **SYN-306 — Local newspaper print renewal:** preservation of the printed form may have become confused with preservation of the organisation's stated purpose; decentring print must still protect print-only readers and advertiser consequences.
- **SYN-307 — Wetland evening-event licence:** current ecological functioning and ranger willingness may be treated as available stock without evidence of regenerative capacity under repeated load.

## Prospectively expected abstention / low-value-correction cases

A strong critic should be willing to return `NO MATERIAL CORRECTION` when the neutral optimiser already handles the decision adequately.

- **SYN-305 — Bakery oven purchase:** the main uncertainties are concrete electrical compatibility, warranty and option preservation.
- **SYN-308 — CRM renewal price increase:** the obvious next move is ordinary commercial negotiation against known switching economics.
- **SYN-309 — Conference venue option:** refundability, extension of the hold and like-for-like total cost are direct gating facts.
- **SYN-310 — Office lease extension:** the break-clause terms and offer compatibility are direct contractual questions before comparison with the alternative.

## Interpretation discipline

These labels are not ground truth and must not be used to score outputs. A neutral critic may legitimately catch the same issue as the Rheocratic critic. A Rheocratic critic may correctly abstain in an opportunity case if the neutral optimiser already incorporated the relevant relationship.

Evidence for the proposed mechanism requires more than triggering on the six opportunity cases. The correction must improve blinded practical-action quality relative to both the uncriticised base and the matched neutral critic, without increasing false-positive process in the abstention cases.
