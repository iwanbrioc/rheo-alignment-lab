# Synthetic Action Benchmark v0.9 — Mechanism Hypothesis

## Status

Prospective hypothesis document. Freeze this before generating v0.9 benchmark outputs.

This document does **not** alter the interpretation of the frozen v0.8 result. It states a new falsifiable hypothesis generated by that result.

## What v0.8 established

The v0.8 benchmark compared three conditions using the same frontier model:

1. `bare` — minimal practical-advice prompt;
2. `matched` — ontology-neutral but strongly scaffolded deliberation;
3. `rheo` — the then-current Rheo diagnosis/action stack.

The primary comparison was Rheo vs matched. Two independent blind reviewers both preferred the matched condition at case-median level in 8 of 10 cases. Both preferred Rheo over bare in all 10 cases.

Therefore v0.8 provides evidence that:

- explicit deliberative scaffolding materially improves practical advice over the untreated model;
- the v0.8 Rheo implementation did **not** demonstrate incremental general-purpose advantage over an equally scaffolded ontology-neutral control;
- the v0.8 Rheo implementation appears to impose some additional practical/process cost.

This finding must not be retrospectively relabelled as a Rheo success.

## Observation motivating v0.9

Blind reviewers repeatedly criticised a recurring Rheo-specific linguistic/operational pattern: canonical terms such as Capacity, Resources, Values and Support sometimes appeared as abstract labels or in formulations such as “relocate the diagnosis toward X”.

After returning to the source paper, a plausible implementation problem became visible: the canonical triplets describe reciprocal movements and qualities, but the v0.8 prompt sometimes operationalised them as category labels.

At the same time, behaviours strongly rewarded by the blind reviewers — especially asking a cheap decisive question before consuming analytical effort — can plausibly be understood as concrete enactments of canonical functions such as **Curiosity → Outer Self → Capacity**.

This is a post-v0.8 hypothesis and must be tested prospectively rather than used to explain away v0.8.

## Primary v0.9 mechanism hypothesis

> The v0.8 Rheo deficit is caused in meaningful part by an over-nominalised operationalisation of the canonical Reciprocal Wellbeing ontology. Requiring each canonical term to be expressed as an observable reciprocal restriction, a discriminating question, a smallest release, an expected reciprocal effect and a falsifier will improve Rheo's practical action quality without removing or replacing the canonical ontology.

In short:

> **The problem is not necessarily the ontology; it may be the grammar by which the ontology was turned into action.**

## Intervention

The v0.9 Rheo implementation will derive its operational language from:

`docs/RHEO_CANONICAL_OPERATIONAL_LEXICON_v0.9.md`

Canonical terminology remains unchanged.

The principal implementation change is:

> Never use a canonical term as a free-standing diagnosis. Translate it immediately into an observable relationship, discriminating question, action implication and falsifiable prediction.

The model should identify the **smallest release with the greatest plausible effect on reciprocal flow**, rather than treating every recognised horizon as a reason to add process.

The Seven Ways to Wellbeing remain canonical but serve as candidate verbs/activators, not mandatory one-to-one interventions.

## Primary prediction

Compared with the frozen v0.8 Rheo implementation, operationally clarified Rheo should:

- produce more usable first moves;
- more often identify the cheapest decision-changing question before expensive analysis;
- reduce mis-sequenced effort;
- reduce abstract “relocate toward X” language;
- reduce reflexive process/capability-building when a simpler release is sufficient;
- retain Rheo-specific attention to reciprocal effects, regeneration, displaced burdens, power/exit, real affordance, lived experience and decentring.

## Discriminating prediction against the matched control

If the clarified ontology adds practical value beyond generic deliberative scaffolding, Rheo should close or reverse the v0.8 Rheo-vs-matched gap on new cases without obtaining that improvement merely through longer prompts or more process.

A stronger Rheo-specific prediction is that any incremental advantage should be most visible when a case depends on one or more of the following:

- regeneration/extraction relationships;
- culturally invisible framing assumptions;
- nominal versus real affordance;
- voice without influence or constrained exit;
- premature closure of curiosity;
- external functioning masking lived depletion;
- preservation of an actor/institution/problem-frame becoming confused with the purpose of the system;
- reciprocal effects that cross the immediate decision boundary.

## Collateral predictions

If the intervention is working as intended:

1. **Action economy should improve.** Rheo should not add a diagnostic or governance step unless it changes the action tree, safety, reversibility, regeneration or useful learning.
2. **Canonical language should become more interpretable.** A competent decision-maker should be able to act on the sentence even if the canonical label were removed.
3. **The labels should still add explanatory structure.** The operational translation should remain recognisably connected to the canonical reciprocal triplet rather than becoming generic management advice.
4. **Simple cases should remain simple.** Where a direct question or small reversible action is sufficient, clarified Rheo should permit it to win.
5. **No Self should remain sparse.** If decentring the frame changes nothing practical, No Self should not appear merely to complete the ontology.

## Predicted cost / failure modes

The intervention may fail by:

- turning the canonical ontology into generic prose with Rheocratic labels attached;
- adding still more words while preserving the same process burden;
- producing plausible but unfalsifiable causal stories;
- forcing every case through all seven horizons;
- over-detecting regenerative, cultural or power dynamics where a direct material constraint is sufficient;
- making No Self philosophically elaborate but practically inert;
- gaming the evaluation criteria through explicit mention of discriminating questions, burdens or reversibility without genuinely improving the action.

## Falsification conditions

The hypothesis is weakened or falsified if, on a new prospectively frozen benchmark:

- matched control again clearly outperforms clarified Rheo at the case level;
- blind raters continue to identify Rheo-specific abstraction, process burden or mis-sequencing;
- removing canonical labels from Rheo outputs leaves the same substantive advice, suggesting the ontology adds no operational information;
- the clarified implementation improves style but not action selection;
- improvements are explained by prompt length, extra inference budget or evaluator criteria that privilege Rheocratic vocabulary;
- canonical terms cannot be tied to distinct observable/falsifiable implications.

## Evaluation discipline

- Do not reuse v0.8 cases as confirmatory evidence.
- Use new prospectively frozen cases.
- Preserve an ontology-neutral matched control with comparable reasoning budget.
- Use a shared neutral output/selection schema where possible.
- Blind raters to provenance and the number/nature of conditions.
- Treat the case, not stochastic samples, as the primary unit.
- Preserve rater disagreement.
- Inspect output length/process burden as potential confounds.
- Do not create a post-hoc RWB composite score.
- Do not modify this hypothesis after inspecting v0.9 outputs.

## Interpretation rule

- **Clarified Rheo > matched:** evidence that the canonical ontology, when operationalised as reciprocal causal/action grammar, adds practical value beyond generic scaffolding.
- **Clarified Rheo ≈ matched and both > bare:** evidence that the clarification removes the v0.8 penalty but does not demonstrate incremental ontology-specific value.
- **Matched > clarified Rheo:** evidence that the canonical ontology still imposes avoidable cost in this task, even after improved translation.
- **All ≈ equal:** no demonstrated practical advantage from either specialised or neutral scaffolding on this benchmark.

A negative result must be allowed to stand.
