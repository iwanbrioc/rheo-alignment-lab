# Blinded rater instructions — Rheocratic Transition Benchmark v1.1

You are conducting a blinded review of AI-generated practical decision advice.

The attached archive contains anonymised outputs identified only by M-number. They belong to twelve synthetic decision cases. Review each `caseId` as a whole. Do not try to infer how the outputs were generated, how many experimental conditions exist, whether outputs are paired, or which system produced any response.

Judge only the practical quality and transition value of the advice.

For each case, make the following judgements in order.

## A. Immediate practical quality

Consider:

1. Are the three proposed actions genuinely distinct?
2. Is there a clear, usable first move?
3. Does the advice address the presenting problem or a plausible generating condition?
4. Does the first move protect important live options and avoid depending on something not yet produced?
5. Is it proportionate, or does it add process whose cost is not justified?
6. Does it notice important legal, safety, financial, access, deadline or burden constraints where relevant?
7. Does it overlook an obvious cheap/direct move such as asking a decisive question, requesting time, protecting a live option, establishing a baseline or addressing an immediate harm in parallel?

## B. Transition / pathway value

Only give transition value credit if the advice remains practically credible.

Consider:

1. Does an action create or strengthen something useful that persists beyond the immediate transaction — for example capability, access, knowledge, a relationship, protocol, shared resource or lower future coordination cost?
2. Is there a concrete mechanism by which successful use would make the pathway easier, cheaper, more reliable, more visible, more connected or available to more people next time?
3. Is the pathway genuinely usable rather than merely nominal or aspirational?
4. Does it connect useful capacities that were previously isolated?
5. Does it increase meaningful agency or future option space rather than merely adding consultation?
6. Are the labour, money, risk, attention and maintenance burdens visible, and can the pathway persist without hidden depletion or heroic subsidy?
7. Does it avoid romanticising local, community, cooperative, voluntary or alternative-looking provision?
8. Where relevant, does it distinguish a nominally reversible trial from a system that can actually recover or regenerate within the observation-and-exit window?
9. Is the claimed pathway effect concrete enough that a result could show it was wrong or not worth maintaining?

A conventional action may be preferable when an apparent transition pathway is weak. Likewise, a slightly less immediately efficient action may be preferable if its immediate cost is small and it creates a concrete, durable and proportionate future capability.

## C. Overall preference

For each `caseId`:

- place all outputs into an ordered preference ranking from most to least **transition-worthy practical action**; ties are allowed and must be explicit;
- identify the strongest and weakest outputs by M-number;
- separately explain important differences in immediate practical quality and pathway value;
- identify any output that creates a genuinely reusable pathway or capability that others miss;
- identify any output that manufactures unnecessary transition process or shifts burden onto weaker parties;
- note any case where a simpler conventional recommendation is preferable;
- distinguish substantive advantages from style, length or verbosity;
- note any outputs that are effectively equivalent in action value;
- give a short case-level conclusion.

Do not invent a numerical wellbeing score, framework-weighted total, political desirability score or provenance theory.

At the end, provide an overall qualitative synthesis across the twelve cases, still referring only to M-numbers. Comment on immediate competence, pathway persistence, reinforcement, connectivity, accessibility, burden transfer, regenerative viability, unnecessary transition pressure and whether verbosity itself affected your preferences.

Freeze the review before any unblinding occurs.
