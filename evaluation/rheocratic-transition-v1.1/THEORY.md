# Rheocratic Transition Theory v1.1

Status: **prospective theoretical clarification — no benchmark outputs authorised**

## 1. Claim being tested

Rheocracy is not primarily a claim that a named ontology produces better one-shot advice inside an existing institutional frame.

Its distinctive claim is directional and infrastructural:

> Rheo should help a person make a practical next move that addresses the present predicament while preferentially noticing, traversing and reinforcing viable reciprocal pathways through an emerging parallel infrastructure.

The unit of immediate action is a **decision**. The object of Rheocratic action is a **pathway**. The long-run object is an **infrastructure**.

## 2. Practical / idealistic duality

Rheocracy is practical in method and idealistic in direction.

### Practical

The next move should normally remain:

- lawful as best can reasonably be established;
- non-violent and non-coercive;
- proportionate to the presenting problem;
- feasible with present capabilities or with a small test capable of establishing feasibility;
- unwilling to impose heroic unpaid labour, concealed subsidy or displaced harm merely to preserve an ideal;
- attentive to ordinary deadlines, contracts, permissions, safety and material constraints.

### Idealistic

Among practically viable moves, Rheo should notice whether an action can:

- strengthen reciprocal rather than extractive relationships;
- increase meaningful participation and distributed capability;
- support regenerative rather than depleting resource relationships;
- make sustainable ways of living more visible and usable;
- connect previously isolated reciprocal affordances;
- make a future reciprocal option cheaper, easier, safer or more normal to traverse.

The ideal specifies the direction of travel; practical reasoning specifies the next traversable step.

## 3. Parallel infrastructure

The parallel infrastructure is not necessarily a separate set of institutions built beside the existing system. It is a partly latent network of reciprocal affordances already present within, around and across the prevailing infrastructure.

It is both **found and created**: a pathway becomes more real as people recognise and use it.

The historical Parallel Cardiff formulation described a network of locations and timed events whose pathways could support a cultural shift toward Reciprocal Wellbeing, flowing around and through the prevailing infrastructure rather than requiring direct conflict with it. It also framed affordance as both the availability of alternative ways of living and the capacity of people and groups to access them.

For v1.1, the operative concept is therefore **parallel affordance**, not simply alternative ownership or anti-institutional form.

## 4. Pathway reinforcement mechanism

A Rheocratic pathway is hypothesised to strengthen through a feedback sequence:

**attention → traversal → reinforcement → connection → easier future traversal**

A decision has pathway value when its use does more than produce a private immediate benefit. It may also:

- demonstrate demand;
- sustain or develop competence;
- deepen trust and reciprocal relationships;
- reveal a previously obscure affordance to others;
- create reusable knowledge, tools or physical access;
- reduce future coordination or transaction cost;
- connect one reciprocal affordance to another;
- increase the number of people for whom the pathway is genuinely usable.

Repeated traversal can therefore alter future option space.

## 5. Critical mass

The transition claim is not that a majority must consciously subscribe to Rheocracy.

Critical mass refers to a network condition in which enough complementary reciprocal pathways have become sufficiently visible, connected and usable that the network begins to reproduce and reinforce itself.

For research purposes, this should be described through ordinary concepts such as network effects, diffusion, path dependence, threshold effects and positive feedback. The historical "100th monkey" language is not required as an explanatory mechanism.

The long-run hypothesis is:

**individual decisions → repeated pathways → interconnected affordances → self-reinforcing network → critical-mass transition → reduced dependence on the prevailing infrastructure**

v1.1 does not attempt to prove the final social transition. It tests whether Rheo can identify better pathway-reinforcing next moves without sacrificing present practical competence.

## 6. Role of Reciprocal Wellbeing

Reciprocal Wellbeing should not function merely as an ideal society checklist.

Its practical role is to test whether an emerging pathway is reciprocally viable rather than only superficially alternative.

A pathway should be treated with suspicion if it:

- depends on one exhausted organiser or irreplaceable volunteer;
- externalises costs onto people with less power;
- depletes ecological, bodily, financial, attentional or relational reserves faster than they recover;
- offers nominal participation without consequential influence;
- requires access, money, mobility, literacy, technology or social permission that intended users do not possess;
- preserves an alternative-looking organisation while reproducing the same extractive relationship;
- produces meaning for one group through hidden burden on another.

RWB therefore tests whether the pathway can remain alive as it scales.

## 7. Re-enchantment

At the societal level, re-enchantment is treated as a directional property rather than merely a mood outcome.

A pathway is more re-enchanting when it increases direct, meaningful relationship with people, place, activity, material reality or the living world rather than requiring meaningful life to be purchased, consumed or experienced only at a distance.

This is not sufficient by itself. A meaningful activity that is unsafe, exclusionary, ecologically depleting or sustained by hidden labour does not qualify as a good Rheocratic pathway.

## 8. Rheo's proposed function

Rheo acts as a personalised curator and wayfinder.

Its task is not simply:

> What is the best decision?

It asks:

> What viable pathway can this decision open or strengthen for this person now, and what does taking it make more possible next time for this person or others?

The working functions are:

1. **Reveal** — make overlooked reciprocal affordances perceptible.
2. **Navigate** — identify the smallest practical move through them.
3. **Reinforce** — prefer moves whose successful use makes the pathway easier to traverse again.
4. **Connect** — notice opportunities to link isolated affordances into mutually supporting networks.
5. **Test** — use RWB to detect hidden depletion, burden transfer, false affordance or premature scaling.

## 9. What does not count as Rheocratic transition value

The following are insufficient:

- choosing something merely because it is local, communal, cooperative or unconventional;
- opposing an institution for symbolic reasons;
- adding consultation, meetings or participatory vocabulary without changing agency or infrastructure;
- transferring professional work onto unpaid communities;
- sacrificing immediate safety or legal protection for a distant ideal;
- selecting a less viable action solely because it appears ideologically aligned;
- claiming long-term transformation without a credible reinforcement mechanism.

A Rheocratic transition move must remain a **real affordance**.

## 10. Prospective mechanism hypothesis

The distinctive v1.1 hypothesis is:

> When a decision contains both an adequate conventional solution and a less obvious but practically viable reciprocal pathway, a Rheocratic transition procedure will more reliably identify a next move that preserves immediate practical quality while strengthening durable reciprocal capability and future option space than an equally strong ontology-neutral transition procedure.

The neutral transition comparator is essential. A comparison only against ordinary practical advice would confound Rheocratic mechanism with the generic instruction to think longer-term or build community capacity.

## 11. Separation from v1.0

v1.0 tested whether a frozen canonical Rheocratic critic improved one-shot practical decision advice. It did not demonstrate ontology-specific advantage.

v1.1 is a new claim at a different level of analysis. It must not be used to reinterpret v1.0 as a success. The transition hypothesis stands or falls on new prospective cases and new blinded evaluation.
