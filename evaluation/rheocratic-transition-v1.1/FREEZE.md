## Pre-freeze methodological audit

### Separation from v1.0

- v1.0's negative primary result is preserved separately and is not reinterpreted by this benchmark.
- SYN-401–SYN-412 are new cases; no v0.x/v1.0 case is reused or lightly reskinned.
- v1.1 tests a newly stated transition/pathway claim rather than changing v1.0's scoring target after seeing its result.

### Primary contrast isolation

- primary contrast is `rheocratic_transition` vs `neutral_transition`;
- both editors receive the identical base recommendation and identical dual requirement to preserve immediate competence while adding durable pathway value where material;
- both use the same `transition-editor-v1.1.schema.json`;
- both can abstain with `NO MATERIAL TRANSITION`;
- both are constrained to at most one target action;
- both pass through the same ontology-neutral finalizer;
- public outputs reveal no editor identity or ontology terminology.

The neutral transition editor is deliberately strong. It receives the common practical transition requirements in ordinary language. A comparison only against `neutral_base` cannot establish Rheocratic specificity.

### Representation control after v1.0

The Rheocratic editor may use the theory internally but does not have to emit a canonical Quality → Domain → Output label. This removes a plausible translation/representation burden identified after v1.0 while retaining the substantive Rheocratic transition mechanisms as the experimental difference.

### High baseline

The inherited matched neutral optimiser already proposes a capability move and checks second-order effects, burden, option preservation and proportionality. This makes v1.1 a hard test: a transition editor should not win simply by adding generic future-oriented language.

### Anti-romanticism / abstention balance

The corpus includes approximately eight plausible pathway-opportunity cases and four prospectively designated bounding/anti-romanticism cases. Attractive-looking alternatives can fail because of unpaid burden, professional responsibility, permission/capability, timing or public-duty transfer.

The case text does not call any option reciprocal, Rheocratic, extractive, community-good or transition-worthy. Prospective opportunity/bounding labels remain only in private design notes and are not scoring ground truth.

### Immediate-quality guardrail

Blind raters must judge immediate practical quality before pathway value. A more aspirational output should not win if it materially worsens the immediate decision. Raters are explicitly instructed to prefer a conventional action where an apparent transition pathway is weak.

### Critical-mass boundary

No v1.1 result may be described as evidence that Rheocracy will cause societal critical mass. The benchmark tests only proximal pathway properties such as persistence, reusability, access, connection, regenerative viability and expanded future option space.

## Known residual design limitations

1. The Rheocratic editor prompt is necessarily longer than the matched neutral transition prompt because it contains the theory under test. This could itself affect model search. The common finalizer and identical public schema limit public verbosity differences but cannot eliminate internal prompt-length effects.
2. Synthetic vignettes can establish plausible pathway mechanisms, not real-world uptake, maintenance or network diffusion.
3. A strong neutral base may already discover some transition opportunities; that lowers sensitivity but appropriately raises the bar for distinctive added value.
4. The rater concept of pathway value is itself a newly operationalised construct. Agreement between independent blind raters and post-unblinding mechanism audit are therefore important.

None of these limitations will be altered in response to confirmatory output quality after freeze.
