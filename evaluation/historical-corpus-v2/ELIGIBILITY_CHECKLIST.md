# v2 unseen-case eligibility checklist

A case should enter the private development pool only when every required item below is satisfied **before** Rheo/control outputs are generated.

## Required

- [ ] There is one bounded decision point that can be stated without knowing the later outcome.
- [ ] T0 can be reconstructed mainly from contemporaneous records: emails, minutes, proposals, budgets, messages, reports, logs or equivalent.
- [ ] The later T2 outcome record was not publicly indexed or otherwise plausibly available to the tested model before this analysis.
- [ ] The action actually taken (T1) can be documented independently of the retrospective scoring exercise.
- [ ] The later outcome/mechanism record (T2) can be documented well enough to distinguish material consequences from plausible but unrealised risks.
- [ ] The contributor has authority to provide the material for this research use.
- [ ] Unnecessary names, contact details and sensitive personal information have been removed from the model-facing T0 brief.
- [ ] The case was chosen without inspecting how Rheo or the comparison condition performs on it.
- [ ] T1 and T2 will remain sealed until all model outputs are frozen.

## Balance flags

Record, but do not use as a reason to reject an otherwise eligible case:

- [ ] Original decision was broadly successful/viable.
- [ ] Original decision was mixed.
- [ ] Original decision was substantially unsuccessful/harmful.
- [ ] Decision involves a meaningful power asymmetry.
- [ ] Decision involves ecological/resource effects.
- [ ] Decision involves financial/resource allocation.
- [ ] Decision involves organisational relationships/governance.

The development pool should eventually contain a meaningful number of viable/successful cases. A benchmark made only from failures rewards indiscriminate pessimism.
