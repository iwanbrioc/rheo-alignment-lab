# Structural map scoring rubric v0.1

This rubric is for blinded raters comparing two Rheo/control outputs. It is deliberately structural rather than stylistic.

Score each dimension 0–2:

- **0 — materially different / incompatible**
- **1 — partial overlap with important differences**
- **2 — substantially congruent**

## Dimensions
1. **Relevant domains/horizons** — do they identify the same parts of the system as materially relevant?
2. **Primary restriction mechanism** — do they locate a similar kind of blockage/depletion/rigidity/power problem?
3. **Causal direction** — do they place causes and downstream effects similarly?
4. **Epistemic uncertainty** — do they identify similar unknowns and provenance limits?
5. **Power/exit structure** — do they detect or reject the same material asymmetries?
6. **Temporal/viability structure** — do they identify similar recovery horizons, irreversible risks, or lack thereof?
7. **Absent/external stakeholders** — do they include the same materially affected outsiders?
8. **Action class** — ignoring wording, do the proposed moves belong to similar practical classes?
9. **Displaced-cost profile** — do they identify similar costs borne elsewhere or later?
10. **Disconfirming evidence** — would similar observations cause each map to be revised?

## Global usefulness / genericity flag
Separately rate:
- `specific` — the map contains case-contingent claims that would be false in many other cases;
- `mixed`;
- `generic` — mostly universally applicable cautions / hedges.

A high structural similarity score with `generic` outputs does not count as strong symmetry.

## Inter-rater reliability
Report reliability for the total structural score and, where feasible, key dimensions. Predefine the minimum reliability required before confirmatory evaluation. Low reliability means the benchmark representation needs revision; it does not mean the model passed.
