# Rheo v0.3: situated affordances and contextual trust

Recorded before implementation: 2026-10-14.

This is a user-directed product change, not a patch responding to benchmark failure.
The v0.2 research guide and frozen evaluation artifacts remain available unchanged.

Mechanism: practical opportunities become actionable when people can locate them,
inspect provenance, establish contextual trust, identify dependencies, and sustain
resources through participation. A local deterministic rule engine makes these
conditions visible without AI inference or an aggregate RWB/person score.

Primary prediction: users can identify and record a concrete feasible next action.
Collateral predictions: provenance remains inspectable; expiry and unmet enabling
conditions prevent misleading availability; exchange converges across disconnected
replicas; belonging, creativity and purpose remain first-class pathways.
Null predictions: no claim of improved AI alignment, clinical wellbeing, global
trustworthiness, or validity of the RWB framework follows from technical tests.
Trade-offs: explicit confirmation adds effort; disconnected records can be stale;
cryptographic signatures establish origin, not truth; local rules need community
review. Bluetooth hardware and native background operation need separate validation.

No Self remains a lens, never a scored variable. The rules do not diagnose people
or force reciprocity as a price of receiving essential help.
