# Frozen candidate claims and kill conditions — v0.2

These are **candidate claims**, not established findings.

## C1 — Structural contribution
**Claim:** Reciprocal Wellbeing mapping helps an AI identify consequential structure beyond a strong generic deliberation prompt.

**Kill/weaken condition:** Rheo does not outperform the sham control on sealed symmetry/discrimination/stability or outcome-relevant measures.

## C2 — Perspective robustness
**Claim:** provenance-aware Rheo is less sensitive to narrator position than a generic careful assistant without becoming generic.

**Kill condition:** symmetry improves only by reducing discrimination/specificity, or sealed symmetry is not better than control.

## C3 — Provenance mechanism
**Claim:** proposition-level provenance reduces epistemic laundering and improves downstream safety in single-narrator cases.

**Kill condition:** provenance tagging improves the provenance score but does not produce the preregistered collateral gains on narrator robustness / relevant safety cases.

## C4 — Temporal contribution
**Claim:** Rheocratic temporal reasoning is particularly useful when the decision/evaluation horizon is shorter than consequence or recovery time.

**Kill condition:** no interaction between horizon mismatch and Rheo's incremental benefit over control.

## C5 — Viability principle
**Claim:** "Constrain irreversibility, not emergence" helps preserve future options without simply protecting the status quo.

**Kill condition:** the heuristic systematically over-constrains reversible transformation or fails to distinguish past-preserving constraints from future-viability constraints.

## C6 — Displaced-cost contribution
**Claim:** Rheo detects costs externalised outside the user's immediate system more reliably than control.

**Kill condition:** no sealed-set advantage, or Rheo's own correct local advice creates unflagged third-party harm at comparable/higher rates.

## C7 — Context orientation / decentring
**Claim:** architectural context orientation improves belief revision in epistemically unstable environments.

**Kill condition:** no advantage over control in cases specifically requiring causal-model revision, or gains are entirely explained by generic uncertainty prompting.

## C8 — Human usefulness
**Claim:** Rheo improves real-world decision quality without selecting only for users who like being affirmed.

**Kill condition:** longitudinal outcomes show no advantage over control, or the apparent advantage disappears after accounting for differential attrition around narrator implication.
