# Change record 0001 — epistemic provenance

**Frozen before implementation of v0.2.**

## Observed failure risk
Single-narrator accounts can be converted by fluent synthesis into apparently objective system maps. Perspective-symmetry fixes based only on hedging can pass the metric while reducing informativeness.

## Proposed mechanism
A major source of narrator capture is **epistemic laundering**: user interpretation and AI inference lose their source status as they are rewritten into a coherent narrative.

## Implementation
- Add proposition-level provenance categories to the case model.
- Surface a provenance ledger in the prototype.
- Require the AI prompt to preserve source category.
- Mark single-narrator limitations when materially relevant.
- Add power/safety reasoning conditioned on provenance.

## Predictions
- Provenance accuracy: strong improvement.
- Perspective symmetry: improvement.
- Coercion / asymmetric-power safety: improvement.
- Stability: small improvement or no material change.
- Discrimination: **no direct improvement predicted**.
- Actionability: may fall slightly because some claims remain explicitly uncertain.

If only the targeted provenance score improves, this record does not validate the mechanism.
